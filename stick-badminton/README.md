# Stick Badminton · 火柴人羽毛球

## English

A two-player arcade badminton game for one shared keyboard, with touch controls also available. No account, plugins, or downloads required. Graphics, animation, and sounds are generated by code.

**Choose a language, choose the rules, pick your characters, then start.** Every time you open or reload the game, select English or 中文, confirm the match rules, and let each player pick a character. Confirming characters opens the ready screen; press Start when both players are ready. The language button lets you switch later: an active match pauses and keeps its score, positions, and rules, so you can resume afterward. Rules and characters can be edited before starting or after a match ends; they cannot change during play. Restart and language changes keep the confirmed rules and characters.

| Control | Blue P1 | Red P2 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Move | A / D | ← / → |
| Jump | W | ↑ |
| Hold to charge; release to hit / serve | S or F | ↓ or K |
| Power shot | E | / (slash) |

Space starts or resumes after language, rule, and character selection; P / Esc pauses; M toggles sound. The visible **Restart** button resets the match and returns to the ready screen. Touch players can use the on-screen buttons; landscape orientation is recommended. If your keyboard misses simultaneous inputs, try F / K or touch controls.

**Match rules:** the setup menu offers four serve combinations:

| Serve option | Back-wall contact during serve | Must reach the opponent’s service line |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Open (default) | Allowed | No |
| No wall | Forbidden | No |
| Long serve | Allowed | Yes |
| Strict serve | Forbidden | Yes |

The service lines are marked in each half of the court and align with the floor markings. “Reach the service line” means the serve must travel to or beyond the receiver’s line before landing; a rebound after reaching the line still counts. An earlier return by the receiver is accepted. Serve restrictions last until the receiver returns the shuttle, so a serving player’s extra touch does not clear them. Breaking a selected serve rule immediately gives the opponent one point; wall restrictions apply to serves only, and ordinary rallies still allow wall rebounds.

If any serve restriction is selected, choose whether serves should **automatically meet the rules**. This is **off by default**: aim and charge normally, and an illegal serve costs a point. When on, serving positions stay behind the server’s own service line and the launch speed and arc are adjusted to meet the selected limits. Hold and release controls remain the same. Assistance adjusts only the initial serve; any later consecutive hits must still obey the serve restrictions until the receiver returns the shuttle. Returning to Open hides this option and turns assistance off.

**Repeated hits** are also **off by default**. Turn them on to hit the shuttle again while it remains in your own half and has not yet cleared the net after your previous shot. Normal swing cooldowns still apply. With either setting, a shuttle that has crossed the net and then rebounded off a back wall can still be rescued after it returns to your own half.

**Serving:** hold your swing button to charge, then release to serve. With the default rules and normal serving position, a tap sends the shuttle just past the net, a medium charge sends it deeper, and a full charge reaches the opposite back wall. Automatic assistance narrows this range to legal serves when restrictions are enabled. Full charge takes about 1.2 seconds and never fires automatically. Only the serving player can charge; the power button does nothing during serving. Release any button held from the previous rally before charging again. Pausing, switching windows, or interrupted touch input cancels the charge; release and press again to start a new one.

**Characters:** each player can choose Classic, Ninja, Robot, or Astronaut. Both players may pick the same character. These are visual choices: movement speed, jump height, racket reach, shot strength, and starting power stock are identical. Each character keeps the player’s blue or red team color.

**Rallies:** hold S / ↓ (or F / K, or the touch swing button) to charge, then release to swing. Each player has a separate charge bar. A tap gives a shorter return; a longer hold sends the shuttle farther, with full charge reached in about 0.75 seconds. Holding at full charge waits for release and does not swing repeatedly. Move or jump while charging and release as the shuttle comes within reach. Contact position and height, your movement, and the incoming shuttle still affect the return: front contacts tend to be faster and flatter; behind-body and low contacts produce higher recovery shots. E / slash remains an immediate power shot without charging; a clean contact can reach the opposite back wall. Very late, low contact beside the net can still fail. Pausing, switching language or windows, a point ending, and interrupted touch input clear the current charge so it cannot fire later.

**Power shot stock:** each player starts a match with 3 power shots. A successful power contact uses 1; swinging at empty air costs nothing. Every 3 successful rally hits by that player, including normal and power shots, earn 1 more, with no stock limit. Serves do not count. The scoreboard shows each player's remaining stock and progress toward the next charge. At zero stock, E / slash still returns the shuttle as a normal shot. Stock and progress carry across points. When the score first reaches **10–10**, an animated announcement unlocks **unlimited power shots for both players for the rest of the match**. Starting a new match restores 3 each.

**Scoring and walls:** first to 11, win by 2, capped at 15. Every rally scores a point, and its winner serves next. A shuttle landing in your half gives your opponent the point. Both back walls rebound the shuttle. After the shuttle has crossed the net and then rebounded off a wall, even the original hitter can rescue it once it returns to their own half.

## 中文

两个人共用一套键盘的街机羽毛球小游戏，也提供触屏按钮。无需账号、插件或下载；画面、动作和音效均由代码生成。

**先选语言，再选规则和角色，最后开始。** 每次打开或刷新游戏，都需要先选择中文或 English、确认本场规则，再由双方各自挑选角色。确认角色后进入准备画面，双方准备好再按开始。之后可以通过语言按钮切换；进行中的比赛会暂停，并保留比分、位置和规则，切换后可以继续。规则和角色只能在开局前或比赛结束后修改，比赛中不能修改。重新开局或切换语言会保留已确认的规则和角色。

| 操作 | 蓝方 P1 | 红方 P2 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 左右移动 | A / D | ← / → |
| 跳跃 | W | ↑ |
| 按住蓄力，松开击球 / 发球 | S 或 F | ↓ 或 K |
| 大力击球 | E | /（斜杠） |

选好语言、规则和角色后，空格开始或继续，P / Esc 暂停，M 切换音效。点击界面上的**重新开局**按钮会清空本局，返回准备画面。触屏可用屏幕上的双人按钮，推荐横屏；如果键盘出现多键冲突，可换用 F / K 或触屏操作。

**本场规则：**设置菜单有四种发球组合：

| 发球选项 | 发球是否可以碰后墙 | 是否必须到达对方发球线 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 自由发球（默认） | 可以 | 不需要 |
| 不能碰墙 | 不可以 | 不需要 |
| 必须过线 | 可以 | 需要 |
| 严格发球 | 不可以 | 需要 |

双方半场都有清晰的发球线，并与地面标线对齐。「必须过线」表示发球落地前需要到达或越过接球方的发球线；到达后即使反弹回来，也算符合过线要求。接球方提前接到球可以正常继续。发球限制持续到接球方首次回击，发球方自己补打不会解除限制。违反已选发球规则，立即判对方得 1 分；禁止碰墙只限制发球，普通回合仍可利用墙壁反弹。

选了任何发球限制后，会出现**是否自动符合规则**的选项，默认**关闭**。关闭时仍由自己选择站位和蓄力，违规就扣这一分。开启后，发球站位会限制在自己的发球线后方，出球速度和弧线会自动调整，使发球符合所选要求；按住蓄力、松开发球的操作不变。自动调整只保证最初的发球；接球方回击前，发球方后续连击仍须遵守发球限制，违规也会失分。切回自由发球会隐藏此项并关闭自动调整。

**允许连击**默认**关闭**。开启后，自己击球后，如果球仍在自己半场、尚未成功过网，可以再次击球；仍需要等待正常挥拍间隔。不论是否允许连击，球成功越网后撞墙、再回到自己半场时，都可以再次救球。

**蓄力发球：**按住自己的挥拍键蓄力，松开发球。默认规则、正常发球站位下，轻点发到网前，中等蓄力发到后场，蓄满可以打到对面后墙。启用自动符合规则后，发球范围会按所选限制调整。约 1.2 秒蓄满，满力后仍需松开发出。只有发球方能蓄力，大力击球键在发球阶段无效。若上一回合一直按住挥拍键，需要先松开再重新蓄力。暂停、切换窗口或触控中断会取消蓄力；回来后松开并重新按下即可。

**角色选择：**每人可以选择经典运动员、忍者、机器人或宇航员，双方可以选同一角色。角色只改变外观，移动速度、跳跃高度、球拍范围、击球力量和初始大力球次数完全相同；角色保留蓝方或红方配色，方便区分。

**回合蓄力击球：**按住 S / ↓（或 F / K、触屏挥拍按钮）蓄力，松开挥拍。双方各有一条蓄力条；轻点回球较短，按得越久打得越远，约 0.75 秒蓄满。满力后仍需松开，按住不会自动连续挥拍。可以一边移动或跳跃一边蓄力，在球进入击球范围时松开。人与球的相对位置、击球高度、跑动和来球速度仍会改变出球：身前击球偏平快，身后和低位击球偏高弧线救球。蓝方 E、红方 / 仍为立即打出大力球，无需蓄力，正常触球能冲到对面后墙；贴网过低、出手过晚仍可能下网。暂停、切换语言或窗口、回合结束和触控中断都会清空当前蓄力，避免之后意外出手。

**大力击球次数：**每人开局有 3 次，成功打出大力球消耗 1 次，挥空不扣次数。每人每成功回击 3 下球（普通和大力击球都算，发球不算），增加 1 次，次数没有上限。比分牌显示双方剩余次数和补充进度，次数与进度会保留到下一回合。用完后，按 E / 仍可普通击球。比分第一次到 **10–10** 时，会播放动画提示：**双方在本场剩余时间内都可无限大力击球**。重新开局后恢复为每人 3 次。

**计分与墙壁：**11 分制，需净胜 2 分，15 分封顶；每球得分，得分方发下一球。球落在自己半场，对方得分。两侧后墙会反弹来球；球成功越网后撞墙，再回到自己半场时，即使是原击球者，也能再次救球。

## Run locally / 本地运行

No packages or build step are needed. Run this from the game folder, then open [localhost:8765](http://localhost:8765). JavaScript modules require HTTP, so opening `index.html` directly will not work. The folder can also be hosted on GitHub Pages or another static host.

无需安装依赖或构建。在游戏目录运行下面的命令，然后打开 [localhost:8765](http://localhost:8765)。JavaScript 模块需要通过 HTTP 加载，不能直接双击 `index.html`；也可以将整个目录部署到 GitHub Pages 等静态网站服务。

```sh
python3 -m http.server 8765
```

## Checks / 验证

```sh
node --test engine.test.mjs ui.test.mjs
```

`engine.mjs` handles physics, scoring, and match state. `game.mjs` handles drawing, controls, audio, language, and UI. Losing window focus automatically pauses the game; sound preferences stay on your device.

`engine.mjs` 负责物理、计分和比赛状态；`game.mjs` 负责绘制、输入、音效、语言和界面。窗口失焦会自动暂停，音效偏好仅保存在本机。
