Worker customization:
worker-upstream.js is the unmodified v0.1.0 integration worker.
worker.js adds versioned Cache Storage for the NNUE, SHA-256 verification,
bounded downloads, progress throttling, and duplicate-init protection.
The modified wrapper is distributed under GPL-3.0-or-later together with its source.

Supported integration:
new Worker('./engine/worker.js')
init message: {type:'init',jsUrl,wasmUrl,netUrl}; use absolute same-origin URLs.
After ready: {type:'command',command:'uci'} (one UCI line per message).
Replies: ready, line, stderr, net-progress, net-written, error, cache-warning.
net-progress includes {loaded,total,cached}; no transferable buffer is required.

Browser limits:
WebAssembly SIMD128, pthreads, SharedArrayBuffer and cross-origin isolation required.
Thread pool is 8; do not set Threads above 8.
WASM memory grows from 256 MiB up to 1 GiB. Keep Hash substantially below that.
Actual speed depends on the device and browser; no native-speed guarantee is made.
